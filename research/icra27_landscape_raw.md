## Evidence synthesis

- **Established/occupied:** global adaptive chunk/horizon; global async chunk execution and stale-context handling; generic slow semantic plus fast force/tactile correction; fixed/nested multi-rate control.
- **No direct work located in this bounded search:** a learned action-chunk executor that (a) measures physical-time validity/recoverability per semantic action subspace, (b) refreshes only the invalid subspace while preserving still-valid native aggregation/commitment, and (c) demonstrates an equal-query-budget benefit across non-contact and contact regimes.
- **Important non-novelty caveat:** semantic group partitioning by itself is not a contribution; action masks or correction heads may turn out to overlap on close inspection. This is a high-risk but testable gap.
