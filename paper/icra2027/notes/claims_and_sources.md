## Wording blacklist for the current draft

Do not claim: statistical significance for `p=.0625`; dynamic group-wise
improvement; a learned scheduler; causal reduction of gripper variation; force
benefits; RoboTwin/RoboDojo/dexterous-hand/real-robot results; generalization
beyond LIBERO Object; state of the art; universal superiority; or a priority
claim such as `the first`.
