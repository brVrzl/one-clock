# Manuscript source migration

Migration date: 2026-08-24.

The active manuscript is now `paper/icra2027/main.tex` with seven numbered
section files:

- `sections/01_introduction.tex`
- `sections/02_related_work.tex`
- `sections/03_method.tex`
- `sections/04_experiments.tex`
- `sections/05_results.tex`
- `sections/06_discussion.tex`
- `sections/07_conclusion.tex`

The prior `main.tex` and its group-specific execution-horizon sections were
retired because they predated the confirmatory asymmetric temporal-reuse
result. The obsolete sources `03_problem.tex`, `04_execution.tex`,
`05_method.tex`, `06_results.tex`, `07_limitations.tex`, and
`08_conclusion.tex` were removed rather than left as a competing paper. The
old `05_experiments.tex` filename was reused, but its contents were completely
replaced and moved to the new `04_experiments.tex` numbering.

Historical notes remain under `paper/icra2027/notes/` for auditability. They
are not included by `main.tex` and must not be treated as current prose unless
reconciled against the verified fact sheet and the post-confirmation reports.

The four active figures are regenerated by `paper/icra2027/generate_figures.py`.
The historical `execution_timeline.pdf`, `results_overview.pdf`, and separate
development plotting scripts were retired. Vector PDF and editable SVG are the
canonical figure formats; PNG files are inspection previews.
