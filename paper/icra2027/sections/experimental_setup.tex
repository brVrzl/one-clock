\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Policy, clock, and action interface}

We evaluate fixed ACT policies~\cite{zhao2023act} using LeRobot v0.4.4
~\cite{cadene2026lerobot} in LIBERO simulation~\cite{liu2023libero}. The
evaluator runs at 20~Hz, so one chunk index is 0.05~s and $d=20$ is 1.00~s.
ACT predicts 100-step chunks without action smoothing. Unless temporal
ensembling is explicitly named, it is disabled. The policy outputs a
seven-dimensional relative end-effector action with the component grouping
defined in Sec.~\ref{sec:same-target}.

\subsection{Cohorts, controls, and inference}

A paired block is one task--initial-state pair evaluated under each compared
condition with matched seeds, preprocessing, step caps, and success criteria.
The primary preregistered cohort contains 140 blocks across five LIBERO-Goal
and five LIBERO-10 tasks. The Object development cohort contains 126 blocks
across nine tasks. Track A contains 450 blocks across LIBERO-10, Goal, and
Spatial.

The dense-query control factorial uses C00 for Fresh arm and gripper, C10 for
H16 arm and Fresh gripper, C01 for Fresh arm and H16 gripper, and C11 for H16
arm and gripper. Every condition queries the policy at each executed step and
discards unused predictions. Success is binary. Paired contrasts use two-sided
exact McNemar tests. We report paired risk-difference intervals and
task-cluster bootstrap intervals already fixed in the canonical analysis.

Primary confirmatory conditions, cohorts, and decision rules were fixed before
outcome inspection. Before selecting held-out confirmation cells, we audited
the available experiment history for prior outcome exposure and excluded cells
with existing outcomes. Development and reviewer-directed characterization are
reported separately rather than pooled with the preregistered primary cohort.
