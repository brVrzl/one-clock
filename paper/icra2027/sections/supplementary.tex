\section{Supporting Interaction Accounting}

Interaction estimates are supporting evidence and are not pooled. In the
original 140-block same-target factorial, the post-study risk-difference
interaction was $+15.71$ points. Its paired-block bootstrap interval was
$[+6.43,+25.00]$, but small-cluster checks were weaker: the task-$t$ interval
was $[-2.76,+34.18]$ and the exact task sign-flip test gave $p=0.101562$. In
the dense-query factorial, the interaction formula was fixed before outcomes
and gave $+9.29$ points, but no interaction-specific interval or $p$-value is
canonical. The Spatial completion has no named canonical interaction estimate.
These point estimates therefore do not establish replicated interaction.
The dense-query estimate was obtained under matched policy-query schedules, so
the observed conditional structure is not created simply by one condition
querying the policy more often than another.
