\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A single temporal execution rule is not behaviorally neutral across ACT action
components. The ordering persists over a broad range of source ages and extends
to translation versus rotation within the arm, while simple same-target
disagreement ranks those components incorrectly. Component-resolved gripper
commitment can improve execution at fixed arm replanning cadence, but coherent
H16 remains the strongest overall operating point. The mechanism and
generality beyond the evaluated ACT policies remain open.
