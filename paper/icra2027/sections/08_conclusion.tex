\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A single execution horizon is a synchronization constraint on every component
of a chunked robot action.  Fixed arm/gripper experiments with a frozen ACT
policy show that relaxing this constraint changes success systematically,
including under matched policy-query rates and across multiple LIBERO Object
tasks.  The present result is a static empirical characterization, not a dynamic
method.  It identifies group-specific horizon assignment---and the consistency
cost of mixed source chunks---as concrete questions for future adaptive
execution.
